

1.BUSINESS PROCESS DESCRIPTION

The selected business process is Medicine Stock Management in a Pharmacy.

This process focuses on managing medicine inventory efficiently, from purchasing and receiving medicines from suppliers to storing, selling, and monitoring available stock.

The proposed Smart Pharmacy Medicine Stock Management System helps pharmacy staff track medicine quantities, monitor expiry dates, receive low-stock and expiry alerts, and predict future medicine demand using historical sales data.

The system aims to reduce medicine waste, prevent stock shortages, improve inventory management, and protect patients from expired medicines.
